# Pattern Name: Private Channel Redirection
**Risk Level:** Medium–High
**Keywords:** DM only, contact privately, WhatsApp rescue, Telegram donation, email for details

## Description
Private channel redirection occurs when a fundraiser or rescue account pushes donors to continue communication outside of public or platform-moderated spaces. This behavior reduces transparency and limits the ability of others to flag inconsistencies or warn potential donors.

## Common Indicators
- **Direct Message Requests:** Instructions to “DM for details” or “message us privately” instead of providing information publicly.
- **Off-Platform Messaging Apps:** Redirection to WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, or personal email addresses for coordination or verification.
- **Disappearing Public Context:** Deletion of public comments asking for proof, or refusal to answer questions in comment sections.
- **Fragmented Communication:** Different information shared privately than what appears in the public fundraiser post.

## Why This Matters
Moving conversations into private channels helps scammers evade platform moderation, avoid public scrutiny, and tailor pressure tactics to individual donors. It also prevents community members from sharing warnings or identifying repeated scam behavior.

## Example Scammer Script
> "For the dog’s safety, please DM us. We can’t share details publicly."

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Legitimate rescues are generally transparent and responsive in public spaces, answering verification questions openly and providing consistent information across platforms. While private messages may be used for follow-ups, core details such as location, vet involvement, and donation methods are publicly accessible.
