# Pattern Name: Untraceable or Direct Payment Requests
**Risk Level:** High
**Keywords:** Venmo friends and family, Zelle only, crypto donation, gift cards, direct wallet transfer

## Description
Untraceable payment requests involve directing donors to payment methods that lack buyer protection, chargeback mechanisms, or institutional oversight. These methods are favored by scammers because once funds are sent, recovery is nearly impossible.

## Common Indicators
- **Personal Payment Handles:** Requests to send money via Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or PayPal “Friends & Family” to individual accounts rather than an official organization.
- **Cryptocurrency or Gift Cards:** Donations requested in crypto wallets, prepaid cards, or vouchers under the guise of “fast” or “international” aid.
- **Platform Bypass:** Claims that fundraising platforms are “too slow,” “blocked,” or “take too much in fees,” followed by a push to pay off-platform.
- **Shifting Payment Targets:** Payment details change mid-conversation, or multiple accounts are suggested without explanation.

## Why This Matters
These payment methods intentionally bypass consumer protection systems and fraud detection tools. Their use removes accountability and makes it extremely difficult for donors to verify where the money goes or recover funds after a scam.

## Example Scammer Script
> "GoFundMe takes too long and the dog doesn’t have time. Please send $150 via Zelle to rescuehelp247@gmail.com right now."

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Legitimate rescues use traceable, auditable donation channels such as GoFundMe, PayPal Goods & Services, or official charity portals tied to registered organizations. They do not pressure donors to use personal accounts or irreversible payment methods.
