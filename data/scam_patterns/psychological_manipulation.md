# Pattern Name: Psychological Manipulation
**Risk Level:** High
**Keywords:** guilt-tripping, graphic suffering, emotional blackmail, shock content, pity appeal

## Description
Psychological manipulation occurs when fundraisers use extreme emotional content to pressure donors into giving. This includes graphic descriptions or images of suffering, appeals to guilt, or framing the donor as personally responsible for the animal’s survival.

## Common Indicators
- **Guilt Appeals:** Language implying moral obligation (“Only you can save her,” “If you don’t help, she will die”).  
- **Graphic Suffering:** Explicit descriptions or images of injury, malnutrition, or distress intended to evoke shock.  
- **Repeated Emotional Pressure:** Multiple posts or messages reinforcing the same emotional narrative to maintain donor engagement.  
- **Moral Framing:** Suggesting that failure to act reflects negatively on the donor’s character or compassion.

## Why This Matters
These tactics exploit human empathy and bypass rational evaluation. Donors experiencing guilt or shock are less likely to verify authenticity or request proof, increasing the likelihood of falling for a scam.

## Example Scammer Script
> "Look at Bella’s ribs showing through her skin. She has been suffering for weeks, and no one else cares. Please help her — she deserves a chance to live."

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Legitimate rescues focus on factual updates, providing clear images and information about treatment or care without exploiting donor emotions. Emotional storytelling is balanced with evidence, transparency, and verifiable documentation.
