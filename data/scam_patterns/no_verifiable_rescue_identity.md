# Pattern Name: No Verifiable Rescue Identity
**Risk Level:** High
**Keywords:** unregistered rescue, no charity ID, missing address, no vet listed

## Description
A lack of verifiable rescue identity occurs when a fundraising entity cannot be independently confirmed as a legitimate animal welfare organization. This includes missing registration details, unverifiable affiliations, or the absence of a real-world operational footprint.

## Common Indicators
- **Missing Legal Registration:** No charity registration number, tax ID, or official nonprofit documentation provided.
- **No Physical Presence:** Absence of a verifiable physical address, shelter location, or publicly listed facility.
- **Unverifiable Veterinary Claims:** Mentions of “our vet” or “partner clinic” without naming a real, searchable veterinary practice.
- **Anonymous Operators:** Fundraisers run by personal accounts with no staff names, leadership information, or organizational history.

## Why This Matters
Legitimate rescues operate within regulatory and professional frameworks that require traceability and accountability. When an organization cannot be independently verified, there is no reliable way to confirm custody of the animal or the intended use of donated funds.

## Example Scammer Script
> "We are a small independent rescue and don’t have official paperwork yet, but the animals need help right now."

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Legitimate rescues clearly display their legal registration, physical location, and veterinary partners, or can provide this information upon request. Even small or grassroots organizations maintain some form of verifiable public record.
