# Pattern Name: Artificial Urgency Deadlines
**Risk Level:** High
**Keywords:** immediate donation, euthanasia threat, 24-hour deadline, urgent appeal, emergency funds

## Description
Artificial urgency deadlines are tactics where fundraisers claim an immediate or imminent threat to an animal’s life to compel rapid donations. Scammers often use fabricated deadlines such as “euthanasia in 2 hours” or “must pay today to save the animal,” pressuring donors to act before verifying authenticity.

## Common Indicators
- **Imminent Threats:** Claims that the animal will be harmed, euthanized, or abandoned within hours or a single day unless funds are received immediately.
- **Pressure Language:** Text emphasizing emergency, panic, or moral obligation (e.g., “time is running out,” “only you can save them now”).
- **Short-Term Deadlines:** Requests for payment within hours, rather than providing a flexible or transparent timeframe.
- **Emotional Framing:** Graphic descriptions of suffering or dramatic imagery accompanying the deadline.

## Why This Matters
Urgency tactics exploit human instinct to act quickly under emotional pressure, bypassing rational verification. Donors are less likely to perform checks or request evidence when faced with a manufactured emergency, increasing scam success rates.

## Example Scammer Script
> "The shelter says if we don’t pay the $500 transport fee by tonight, Max will be euthanized. Please donate immediately via Zelle!"

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Legitimate rescues provide realistic timelines and do not pressure donors with fabricated deadlines. Emergency appeals are usually accompanied by verifiable proof, transparent procedures, and official channels, and they allow donors time to confirm details or consult with authorities.
