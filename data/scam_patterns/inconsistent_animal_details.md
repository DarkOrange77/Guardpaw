# Pattern Name: Inconsistent Animal Details
**Risk Level:** Medium–High
**Keywords:** age mismatch, breed change, location conflict, story inconsistency, conflicting animal details

## Description
Inconsistent animal details occur when the information provided about an animal varies across posts, updates, or fundraising messages. This includes changes in age, breed, location, medical condition, or even the animal's name. Such contradictions often indicate recycled stories, fabricated rescues, or careless copy-pasting.

## Common Indicators
- **Age/Breed Mismatch:** The same animal is described as a puppy in one post and an adult dog in another, or the breed changes without explanation.
- **Location Conflicts:** The reported location of the animal shifts between posts, or differs from the fundraising platform’s declared region.
- **Story Variations:** Injury, adoption history, or backstory changes between updates or across social media platforms.
- **No Follow-Up Evidence:** Lack of consistent photographic or video evidence supporting the animal’s ongoing status.

## Why This Matters
Legitimate rescues maintain accurate, time-consistent information. Inconsistent details are strong signals of low authenticity and can indicate that media or stories have been reused, animals have been swapped, or the campaign is entirely fabricated.

## Example Scammer Script
> Post 1: "Luna, a 6-month-old golden retriever, was found abandoned on Main Street."  
> Post 2 (later): "Luna, now 1 year old, a Labrador mix, still waiting for rescue!"  

## How Legitimate Rescues Differ
Real rescues carefully document animal details and provide consistent updates across platforms. Changes in the animal’s status, age, or story are explicitly explained and supported by verifiable photos, vet reports, or adoption records.
