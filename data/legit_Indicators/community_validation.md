# Indicator Name: Community Validation
**Confidence Level:** High
**Keywords:** volunteer references, adopter feedback, local shelter, tagged posts, community engagement

## Description
Community validation occurs when a rescue or fundraiser demonstrates authentic engagement with its local community, volunteers, adopters, or other stakeholders. This includes references, public posts, or verifiable interactions that confirm the legitimacy of the rescue.

## Common Indicators
- **Volunteer or Staff References:** Donors can verify animal care through contact with active volunteers or rescue staff.  
- **Adopter Feedback:** Positive, traceable feedback from previous adopters or beneficiaries is publicly available.  
- **Local Shelter Verification:** Rescue is registered with or affiliated to local shelters, veterinary clinics, or recognized organizations.  
- **Tagged Posts or Public Updates:** Photos or updates include tagged community members, events, or locations that can be cross-verified.

## Why This Matters
Scammers often operate anonymously or avoid interactions with local communities. Community validation provides **traceable evidence** of a rescue’s legitimacy and increases donor confidence by showing consistent, verifiable social proof.

## Example Positive Scenario
> "Fido’s adoption post includes comments from previous adopters and is tagged with our registered local shelter. Volunteers can be contacted to confirm his care."

## How Scams Differ
Fraudulent fundraisers typically avoid providing verifiable contacts, do not engage local communities, and lack adoption history or feedback. Requests for donations are often made without any social proof or traceable presence.
