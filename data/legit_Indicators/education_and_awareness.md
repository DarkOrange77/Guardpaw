# Indicator Name: Education and Awareness
**Confidence Level:** Medium–High
**Keywords:** BBB tips, law enforcement, public guidance, scam prevention, informed donor

## Description
Education and awareness occurs when donors, adopters, and the general public are informed about common scams, best practices, and regulatory guidance. This includes following BBB advice, understanding warning signs, and sharing knowledge to prevent fraud.

## Common Indicators
- **BBB Guidelines:** Donors follow recommendations such as meeting the animal, verifying sellers, or using secure platforms.  
- **Law Enforcement Advice:** Awareness of reporting procedures or ongoing investigations related to online pet scams.  
- **Community Sharing:** Donors, volunteers, or media share tips or studies about how to identify fraudulent campaigns.  
- **Preventive Actions:** Use of background research, reverse image searches, and verification before donating.

## Why This Matters
Educated donors are less likely to fall for scams. Awareness of common tactics and preventive measures acts as an early warning system and supports safer transactions.

## Example Positive Scenario
> "The BBB recommends meeting the pet via video call or in person and verifying any adoption fees through official channels before sending money."

## How Scams Differ
Fraudulent fundraisers often discourage independent verification and exploit donors who are unaware of common scam tactics. They rarely provide educational resources or guidance to ensure transparency.
