# Indicator Name: Physical Verification
**Confidence Level:** High
**Keywords:** see in person, video call, meet the animal, verify location, virtual inspection

## Description
Physical verification occurs when a donor or adopter can confirm the existence of the animal and the legitimacy of the rescue organization through direct observation. This includes in-person visits or secure video calls with the seller or shelter.

## Common Indicators
- **In-Person Meetings:** The organization allows potential donors to visit the shelter or rescue location to see the animal firsthand.  
- **Video Calls / Virtual Tours:** Modern rescues offer real-time video calls or live-streams to verify the animal and the rescue staff.  
- **Documented Confirmation:** The organization provides verifiable records during the visit, such as adoption paperwork, veterinary receipts, or identification tags.  
- **Transparency in Communication:** Rescue staff answer questions openly during meetings or calls without pressuring the donor.

## Why This Matters
Physical verification ensures that the animal exists and is in the care of a legitimate organization. Scammers are unlikely to allow such direct observation because it would expose the fraud. This indicator significantly reduces risk and increases donor confidence.

## Example Positive Scenario
> "We set up a 15-minute video call so you can meet Max in his foster home and see his condition. All veterinary documents will be shared during the call."

## How Scams Differ
Fraudulent fundraisers rarely allow in-person visits or video verification. They often insist on off-platform communication, personal payment, or provide vague excuses for avoiding direct observation.
