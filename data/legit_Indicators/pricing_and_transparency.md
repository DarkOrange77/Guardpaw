# Indicator Name: Pricing and Transparency
**Confidence Level:** High
**Keywords:** fair price, market rate, transparent fees, cost verification, reasonable adoption

## Description
Pricing and transparency occurs when a rescue or fundraiser provides realistic, verifiable pricing for animals or adoption fees. This includes clearly stating adoption costs, veterinary fees, and additional expenses. Transparent pricing reduces the likelihood of scams using “too good to be true” offers.

## Common Indicators
- **Realistic Adoption Fees:** Prices align with typical market rates for the breed, age, or medical care required.  
- **Transparent Breakdown:** Rescue provides clear information on what the fee covers (e.g., vaccinations, microchipping, foster care).  
- **No Hidden Charges:** Donors are not asked for untraceable payments, secret shipping fees, or emergency “top-up” donations.  
- **Comparative Research:** Rescue’s pricing is consistent with local shelters or reputable breed-specific organizations.

## Why This Matters
Unrealistically low prices or hidden fees are common in fraudulent campaigns. Verifiable and transparent pricing indicates professionalism, regulatory compliance, and legitimacy, making it easier for donors to trust the organization.

## Example Positive Scenario
> "Adoption fee is $200, covering vaccines and microchipping. Shipping costs are optional and documented, and all payments are through the GoFundMe portal."

## How Scams Differ
Scams often advertise free or extremely cheap animals to attract impulse donors. They may follow up with multiple “hidden” fees or request payments via untraceable methods like Zelle, Venmo, or crypto.
