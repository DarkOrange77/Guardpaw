# Indicator Name: Media Verification
**Confidence Level:** High
**Keywords:** reverse image search, original photo, authentic media, unique images, verified pictures

## Description
Media verification occurs when images of the animal or rescue organization can be independently confirmed as original and authentic. Donors or adopters can use reverse image searches or distinctive visual cues to ensure photos are not stolen, reused, or manipulated.

## Common Indicators
- **Reverse Image Search:** Photos uploaded by the rescue do not appear elsewhere on the web or stock image databases.  
- **Distinctive Features:** Unique collars, markings, or background elements in photos that match the reported location or animal.  
- **Consistent Media Updates:** New images or videos over time showing growth, healing, or ongoing care of the animal.  
- **Source Verification:** Photos provided by the rescue can be traced to credible staff or volunteers.

## Why This Matters
Scammers often reuse stock images, stolen photos, or pictures from other rescues to fabricate campaigns. Verifiable media ensures the animal exists and is being cared for by a legitimate organization. This indicator increases donor confidence and reduces fraud risk.

## Example Positive Scenario
> "We uploaded photos of Bella each week with her unique collar and background. A reverse image search confirms these images do not appear elsewhere online."

## How Scams Differ
Fraudulent campaigns frequently use stock images, recycled photos, or images from other rescues. They often resist requests for additional, verifiable media or live photos.
